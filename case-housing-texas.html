Case Study: Texas Housing Market

How have apartment rents evolved across major Texas cities post-pandemic?

🏙️ Problem

As Texas cities grow rapidly, renters face unpredictable cost changes. We wanted to uncover city-level rent patterns from 2019–2024.

📊 Approach

Cleaned and analyzed rental listings data from Apartment List, Zillow, and Rent.com. Used Python (Pandas, Seaborn) for time-series analysis and geographic comparisons. Focused on Austin, Dallas, Houston, and San Antonio.

📈 Outcome

Found that Austin experienced the sharpest post-2021 rent spike (+34%), while Houston remained more stable. Built Tableau dashboards to visualize monthly trends and YOY changes.

🔍 Skills Highlighted

  • Data wrangling (multi-source CSVs, outlier filtering)
  • Time-series trend analysis & rolling averages
  • Storytelling via interactive Tableau dashboards
  • Geo segmentation and KPI benchmarks
