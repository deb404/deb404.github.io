Case Study: Circular Fashion Modeling

Data meets conscious closets.

👗 Problem

How can we quantify the lifecycle and reuse value of clothing to promote sustainable choices?

📊 Approach

Built an Excel-based cost + environmental impact model based on consumer surveys and resale price tracking from Depop and Vinted.

🌍 Outcome

Identified key cost-saving and environmental benefits of reuse. Visuals used by a fashion NGO campaign.
