Case Study: Coordinating Elder Care

Data and design for better aging support.

🧓 Problem

Families struggle to coordinate health and home care for aging relatives. There’s no centralized tool.

📋 Approach

Mapped user needs and created wireframes. Built a Figma prototype and data model (Google Sheets → Airtable).

🛠️ Outcome

Tested with 5 users. Prioritized calendar syncing, medication reminders, and secure sharing. Next: MVP build.
